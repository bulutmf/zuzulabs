# Privacy Policy for Math Warrior

**Last Updated:** October 2026

Zuzu Lab ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and the privacy of your children. We created *Math Warrior* to be a safe, educational way for children from Kindergarten to Grade 5 to practice math. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices.

## 1. Privacy

* **We do not collect, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII).**
* **We do not track location data.**
* **We do not use third-party analytics or advertising networks.**
* **The app does not create accounts and does not require you to sign in.**
* **The app makes no network requests.** All content ships inside the app, so *Math Warrior* works fully offline and has no servers to send your information to.

## 2. Local Data Storage

To remember each child's progress, the app saves the following on your device only: the names, grades, and avatars you enter for each child; their stars, coins, levels, practice history, and settings; and a scrambled (salted SHA-256) copy of the parent passcode — never the passcode itself. This data is stored using Apple's native frameworks (UserDefaults and the app's own storage). We have no access to it, and it is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

If your device is backed up to iCloud or a computer, this data is included in that backup like other app data; backups are handled by Apple under your own device settings.

You can reset a child's progress or delete a child's profile in the parent area. Deleting the app permanently removes everything it saved.

## 3. Reading Aloud

When "Read questions aloud" is on, the app speaks using Apple's built-in text-to-speech on your device. No audio is recorded, and the microphone is never used.

## 4. In-App Purchases and Links

*Math Warrior* contains no in-app purchases, no advertising, no subscriptions, and no external links. The coins children earn are play rewards inside the game only. Settings and progress reports are kept behind a parent passcode.

## 5. Children's Privacy

*Math Warrior* is made for children from Kindergarten to Grade 5. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date above and post the revised version at this address.

## 7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our practices, please contact us.
